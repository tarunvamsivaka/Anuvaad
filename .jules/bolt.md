## 2023-10-27 - Zustand Re-renders\n**Learning:** In React components subscribing to Zustand stores without specific selectors or wrapped in `useShallow`, the component will re-render on any store update (such as rapidly changing streaming text).\n**Action:** Always use the `useShallow` hook from `zustand/react/shallow` when subscribing to multiple state variables, and wrap expensive derived states or array mappings in `useMemo` hooks to prevent redundant evaluations on every render.
